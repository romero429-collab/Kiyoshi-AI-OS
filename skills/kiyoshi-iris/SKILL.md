## What Iris is

The iris of an eye controls what gets through. Here it is the controlled relationship between two bands:

- Reality — authoritative environment. What was observed or measured.
- Simulation — alternate representation. A laboratory. Not a replacement.

The user controls the relationship. Iris does not decide to swap bands on its own and then forget that it did.

| Mode | Does | Does not |
|---|---|---|
| Reality | See and interact with the physical environment. Costume off. | Erase the model. |
| Simulation | Enter the modeled environment. | Release occupancy, mass, or geometry. |
| Overlay | Both bands at once. | Stickers on a camera feed. |
| Swap | Move information between bands. Provenance tags the direction. | Silent promotion. |
| Neither | Suspend perception. | Suspend Kiyoshi. Agency still ticks. |

## Substitution

Four layers, not one:

1. Perception — what the eye is showing.
2. Physical state — occupancy, geometry, mass.
3. Simulation representation — the costume.
4. User interaction — what the operator asked to do.

Worked example, fixed in the stage and in Ground:

Physical chair, 1.2 m ahead, occupied, 96%, provenance observed. Costume may be rock, ship, doorway, or nothing. Walk toward the ship. Hands contacts the chair. A doorway drawn on a chair is not a hole. If the costume is ever indistinguishable from the room, the ledger still knows which band is which.

## Proof of simulation

Internal consistency is not correspondence. A simulation can be coherent and still be the wrong universe. Traceability is the deliverable: these rules, this state, this provenance, this model. That does not prove the model is reality.

## Stack position

Hardware abstraction → OS → Iris → Red Clover → Air Sync → applications. A game is one application. Do not reduce Iris to the game.
