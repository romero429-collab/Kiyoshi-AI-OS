# OSINT Multi-Domain Live Feeds DDS Skill

Maps heterogeneous live sources (Hikvision RTSP cameras with SCRFD/ArcFace, ADS-B planes, AIS ships, satellite feeds) to a product discrete dynamical system. Verifies contraction of tracking residuals and activation of unified single-screen monitoring via negative largest Lyapunov exponent.

## Trigger

Requests for OSINT tools, multi-domain live tracking, unified dashboard of cameras/planes/ships/satellites, or extensions of surveillance systems to heterogeneous feeds.

## Output Contract

Kebab-cased directory with README, MVP, MK, MV, phase-space-visualizer.html. Claims grounded in λ₁ < 0 and spectral radius of residual map < 1.
