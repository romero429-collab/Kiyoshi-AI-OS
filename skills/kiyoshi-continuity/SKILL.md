# Continuity

Source: transcripts [09](../kiyoshi-conversation/references/transcript/09-gravity-not-magnetism.md) through [15](../kiyoshi-conversation/references/transcript/15-model-under-test.md), [19](../kiyoshi-conversation/references/transcript/19-keep-versus-hold.md), [20](../kiyoshi-conversation/references/transcript/20-continuity-engine.md). Code: `DOMAINS`, `PRIMITIVES`, `PROVENANCE`, `ENVELOPES`, `CONTINUITY`, `RIGOR` in `field-data.ts`.

## Heart sentence

Kiyoshi does not assume that its representation of reality is reality. It continuously maintains the relationship between observation, representation, simulation, and action.

## Chain

Prior → Encounter → Perceive → Evidence → Interpret → Act → Consequence → Revise.

Reality → Kiyoshi → Iris → Kiyoshi → Reality.

Memory is that chain, not a chat log. A record holds: what happened, what was perceived, what was believed, confidence, the action, the consequence, the model in force, the substrate, the active constraints, what the user permitted, and what the discrepancy taught. Simulation history and physical history stay connected and unconfused.

## Five primitives

State. Entity. Environment. Representation. Transition.

Agents, maps, robots, games, BCIs are applications of those. They are not a new ontology each time.

## Seven domains

| Domain | Who | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Air Sync | Matter, energy, space, sensors. |
| Biological | Anise / Cybercell | Cells, signals, adaptation. Not nervous-system cosplay. |
| Computational | Kiyoshi Core | State, memory, processing. |
| Intelligence | Agents | Reasoning, perception, agency, learning. Inside the architecture. |
| Simulation | Iris | Models. A laboratory. |
| Network | Red Clover | Who has the turn. Claim, speak, yield. |
| Human / social | Blue Lotus | Identity, access, rules. |

Red Clover moves signals → state → decisions → permissions → actions → feedback. Ten specialists do not simultaneously decide they may act.

## Envelopes

Reality (not negotiable) → system (charter, permissions) → substrate (what this body can do) → user (overlays, autonomy, modes) → learned (what experience changed).

Phone, robot, and BCI are bindings. Kiyoshi is not phone software.

## Scale

Particle → atom → material → furniture → room → house → environment. A new name at a new scale is a new organization, not a disconnected substance. One skull does not hold the whole. Wholeity is the system, achieved by coordination.

## Keep

Relationships (who has the turn, who owns state, who yields, which mode, what a miss does). Iris as a relationship, not VR. Substrate-independent interfaces. Explicit coordination. Obsessive provenance. Five primitives. Continuity of state.

## Hold — hypothesis, never law

- Gravity = magnetism. Electricity and magnetism are one field; gravity is curvature of spacetime until a measurement says otherwise. Encode “Hypothesis: gravitational phenomena may be a deeper relative of electromagnetic phenomena.” If it is right, evidence accumulates. If it is wrong, the miss is locatable. Gabriel’s unification intuition (transcript 10) is the initial model, not the final authority.
- One monolith. It should feel like one system. Internally the pieces evolve separately.
- AGI as the first objective. First objective: substrate-independent interoperability that can represent, simulate, coordinate, and interact. Intelligence runs inside that.
- Costume leaking into occupancy.

## Physics check before you write a law

A claim becomes an article only with a measurement or a derivation from one. A rhyme across scales is a hypothesis. Local consistency does not promote to global correspondence (Article V). Article IV still requires a verification suite before a new subsystem is accepted.
