# Beacon Academy Economic Mechanics — Detailed Development

## Focus: Retirement Liquidity Pool Mechanics

### 1. Purpose
The Retirement Liquidity Pool is the primary mechanism that converts lifetime contribution (Primary Role performance + successful passion projects) into tangible quality-of-life improvements during retirement. It embodies the principle that "time is currency" and rewards long-term value creation for the colony.

### 2. Funding Model

**Base Funding Rate**
- A fixed **8–12%** of total colony production (goods, services, innovation value, resource extraction) is automatically diverted into the Retirement Liquidity Pool every rhythmic information wave cycle (weekly at Academy scale).
- This rate is adjustable via Colony-level Adaption Report + democratic vote, but changes require a supermajority and must include sustainability modeling from the Digital Twin.

**Dynamic Adjustment**
- If the pool balance falls below 18 months of projected draw demand, the funding rate automatically increases by 1% per cycle until the buffer is restored (capped at 15%).
- If the pool exceeds 5 years of projected demand, the funding rate can be temporarily reduced by up to 2% via Adaption Report proposal (subject to vote).

### 3. Draw Rights Calculation

**Base Retirement Entitlement**
Every citizen who reaches age 59½ (or earlier via earned retirement points) receives a **Base Draw Right** calculated as:

Base Draw = (Total Career Contribution Points × Pool Draw Multiplier) / Expected Retirement Years

Where:
- **Total Career Contribution Points** = Primary Role performance score + Passion Project success score (weighted by colony benefit) + Mentorship/Leadership contribution.
- **Pool Draw Multiplier** is determined by the current pool health and colony productivity level.
- Expected Retirement Years = 25 (age 59½ to ~85, with flexibility).

**Performance Bonus**
Citizens in the top 20% of Fitness Function (Fᵢ) during their working life receive an additional **Performance Bonus** (up to +40% on base draw). This is funded from the same pool but is capped so it does not deplete the base entitlement for average performers.

**Need-Based Supplement**
During periods of personal or family stress (e.g., caring for elderly parents, health issues), retirees can apply for temporary supplements. These are approved through the Chain Link Mentorship system and drawn from a small emergency sub-pool (5% of total Retirement Liquidity Pool).

### 4. Sustainability & Anti-Depletion Rules

- The pool is **never allowed to drop below 12 months** of projected draw demand. If it approaches this floor, automatic measures activate:
  - Temporary reduction in Performance Bonus payouts.
  - Increased funding rate (as described above).
  - Prioritization of base entitlements over bonuses.
- Large one-time draws (e.g., for major housing upgrades or medical needs) require approval from the retiree's Development Link mentor chain and are logged on the ledger.
- The Digital Twin continuously runs sustainability simulations. If projected depletion risk exceeds 15% within 10 years, an Adaption Report is automatically generated for Colony-level review.

### 5. Integration with Chain Link Mentorship & Resilience

- Retirement points and draw rights are recorded as part of the persistent Development Link state.
- During Tier 2+ resilience events (mass loss), the system can temporarily redirect a portion of the Retirement Liquidity Pool toward emergency support for surviving high-fitness fragments (with mandatory replenishment after recovery).
- This ensures the pool supports both individual retirement and civilizational survival.

### 6. Anti-Gaming Protections

- All contribution scoring is derived from the existing Chain Link progress reports and Fitness Function — not self-reported.
- Passion project success must demonstrate measurable colony benefit (validated through Adaption Report feedback or peer review within the mentorship chain).
- Large or suspicious contribution spikes trigger automatic audit by the mentor chain above the individual.

This model ensures the Retirement Liquidity Pool is sustainable, fair, and deeply aligned with the biological reinforcement + pruning philosophy of Beacon Academy.
