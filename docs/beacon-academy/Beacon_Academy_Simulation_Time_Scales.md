# Beacon Academy Digital Twin — Simulation Time Scales

## Core Principle
The Digital Twin must support multiple time scales simultaneously to be useful for both short-term operational decisions and long-term civilizational planning. All scales are designed to be **composable** within the same Hybrid Shadow Twin architecture.

## Recommended Time Scales

| Scale Name              | Real-World Equivalent | Simulation Speed          | Primary Use Cases                                                                 | Trigger / Activation                          |
|-------------------------|-----------------------|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| **Real-Time Shadow**    | 1:1                   | Real time (or slight lag) | Early warning, divergence detection, live monitoring of rhythmic waves and trigger status | Always running alongside live system         |
| **Tactical**            | 1 day – 4 weeks       | 10x – 100x accelerated    | Testing short-term policy changes, temporary split outcomes, crisis response routing | Ad-hoc or triggered by Tier 1/2 events       |
| **Operational**         | 1–6 months            | 500x – 2,000x accelerated | Economic mechanics validation, retirement pool sustainability checks, Network-level fission testing | Scheduled weekly + on major Adaption Reports |
| **Strategic**           | 1–5 years             | 5,000x – 20,000x accelerated | Long-term resilience modeling, Colony budding scenarios, Fitness Function optimization, rule evolution | Scheduled monthly + on Tier 2+ triggers      |
| **Generational**        | 10–50 years           | 50,000x+ accelerated      | Multi-generational impact of economic rules, pruning thresholds, and biological principle integration | Scheduled quarterly + on Colony-level votes  |
| **Existential**         | 50–200+ years         | Maximum acceleration      | Full Colony collapse/recovery scenarios, long-term sustainability of Retirement Liquidity Pool, civilizational phase transitions | Only on Tier 4 triggers or explicit Colony quorum request |

## Implementation Notes

- **Hybrid Shadow Twin** runs the Real-Time Shadow continuously.
- Accelerated branches are spawned on demand from the Shadow state (preserving current Development Link topology, Fitness distribution, and trigger status).
- All accelerated simulations include stochastic noise modeling to reflect real-world uncertainty.
- Results from accelerated branches are automatically compared against the live system's trajectory. Significant divergence triggers an Adaption Report.

## Connection to Section 13 Resilience

- **Tier 1 & 2 events**: Primarily use Tactical and Operational scales for rapid response planning.
- **Tier 3 (Mass Loss / Fission)**: Requires Strategic scale simulations to validate budding strategies and morphological memory backup effectiveness.
- **Tier 4 (Existential Crisis)**: Triggers Existential scale simulations to explore long-term recovery or graceful degradation paths.

This multi-scale approach ensures Beacon Academy can make decisions with appropriate foresight at every level of urgency while maintaining the biological self-healing principles at the core of the system.
