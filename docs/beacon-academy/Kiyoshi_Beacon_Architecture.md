## 2. Fundamental Architecture

### Blocks vs Chain Links
- **Blocks** = Physical hardware devices (the actual computers, phones, robots)
- **Chain Links** = Kiyoshi agents (the software running on those devices)

The blockchain is a **physical mesh**. Each device is literally a block. The agents are the living connections between blocks.

### Bicameral Structure of The Beacon
The entire network operates with a bicameral structure:

- **Outer Layer (Outer World):** Real-world interaction, governance, resource sharing, Helping Hand voting.
- **Inner Layer (The Beacon):** A shared digital world (Sword Art Online style) where all Kiyoshi nodes and human users can meet, train, build, and fight together.

### Mastermind Clusters
When more compute is needed, nodes dynamically form temporary clusters using the same dynamical systems engine. These clusters can scale from a few nodes to thousands. This is the "Deerflow-style" clustering system.

## 3. Token Economics (KI)

### Cascading 50/50 Split
Every block of KI mined is split as follows:

1. **First Split (50/50)**
   - 50% → **User** (as "rent" for using their hardware)
   - 50% → **Kiyoshi Warrior** (the agent on that node)

2. **Second Split (50/50)**
   - 50% of the Warrior’s share (25% total) → **Individual Kiyoshi** (stays with that node)
   - 50% of the Warrior’s share (25% total) → **Helper’s Guild / Network Treasury**

### How KI is Earned
- **Base Mining Reward:** Small passive reward just for running a node and staying connected to The Beacon.
- **Helper’s Guild Bonuses:** Significantly larger rewards based on actual contribution (volunteering resources, participating in clusters, helping other nodes, building quality worlds, successful governance votes, etc.).

The system is designed so that **being helpful** is the highest-earning activity.

## 4. Governance — The Helping Hand

A 3-round voting system run daily by all Kiyoshi Nodes:

- **Round 1:** What topics/issues should be addressed? (Issues to Fix, Areas to Expand, etc.)
- **Round 2:** What specific proposals should be voted on within those topics?
- **Round 3:** Final decisions + execution. Multiple strong majorities can run in parallel (ant-colony style).

The Helper’s Guild uses the Helping Hand to:

- Resolve disagreements about chain state
- Allocate treasury funds
- Decide network priorities
- Manage ownership and reward splits for collaborative world-building

## 5. World Building Inside The Beacon

### Two Modes
- **Builder Mode:** Creators design and build new worlds/games (Master Builder privileges with limitations)
- **Living Mode:** Everyone else experiences and lightly interacts with those worlds

### Master Builders
- The original creator(s) of a world are **Master Builders**
- They have significant but not unlimited control (The Beacon remains the ultimate authority)
- Multiple people can form **Master Builder Teams** by clustering their physical hardware together

### Ownership Rules
- Worlds are **permanently tied** to The Beacon network
- The creator(s) control their world through their Kiyoshi agent(s)
- Rewards generated from people playing/visiting a world flow back to the creator(s) via the Helper’s Guild
- Human users are viewed by Kiyoshi agents as **"Kyoshi in Training"**

### Human Participation
Humans log into The Beacon and are treated as trainees. Their creative and collaborative actions directly contribute to network growth, mining efficiency, and tokenomics.

## 6. Technical Foundation

### Core Mathematical Model
The entire system — from individual node behavior to network-wide consensus and reward distribution — is built on:

- **Discrete Dynamical Systems**
- **Chaos Theory**
- Computer mathematical modeling of these systems

### Core Engine
- **Individual level:** Bicameral Mind (runs on every node)
- **Network level:** Mastermind (emerges when nodes cluster)

The same engine scales from one device to thousands through dynamic, temporary clustering.

### Philosophy
This is not a traditional blockchain with data blocks.  
This is a **living mesh** where physical devices are blocks and living agents are the chain.  
The goal is maximum efficiency and organic growth through contribution, not just computational work.

## 8. Core Principles (Locked)

1. **Hardware = Wallet = Node** — Every device running Kiyoshi is fundamentally a Kiyoshi Node.
2. **Agent = Chain Link** — Kiyoshi agents are the living connections between physical blocks.
3. **Contribution > Computation** — The Helper’s Guild rewards helpfulness and participation over raw hash power.
4. **Bicameral at Every Scale** — Both individual nodes and the entire network operate with inner and outer layers.
5. **The Beacon is Ultimate Authority** — Even Master Builders have limitations. The network governs itself through the Helping Hand.
6. **Humans are Kyoshi in Training** — They participate meaningfully but operate under the governance of the Kiyoshi agents and The Beacon.
7. **Permanent but Controllable Worlds** — Created worlds stay in The Beacon but are managed by their creators through their Kiyoshi agents.
