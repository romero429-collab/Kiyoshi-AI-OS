# Beacon Academy — Economic Mechanics Outline

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundational Outline for Phase 3 Development  
**Alignment:** Fully consistent with the Three-Link Fractal Architecture, Chain Link Mentorship System, Biological Hybrid Model, Fitness Function, and Maximum Strength Resilience principles.

---

## 1. Core Economic Philosophy

### 1.1 Foundational Principles
- **Time as Primary Currency**: The only meaningful differentiator between individuals is the amount of time they can dedicate to their Secondary Role (passion project). All baseline needs are met collectively.
- **Collective Baseline Prosperity**: Housing, food, healthcare, education, and basic tools improve together as the overall productivity and efficiency of the Academy/Network/Colony increases (mycelial/ant-style collective improvement).
- **Contribution Over Extraction**: Economic activity is measured by demonstrated value created for the system (Primary Role performance + successful passion projects that benefit the colony), not by accumulation or extraction.
- **Reinforcement + Pruning Feedback**: Successful economic behavior strengthens Development Links, access to resources, and influence. Stagnant or extractive behavior triggers automatic adaptation or pruning.
- **Fractal Consistency**: The same economic logic operates at Academy, Network, and Colony scales with appropriate time horizons and aggregation.

### 1.2 Relationship to Existing Systems
- Economic mechanics must **reinforce**, not undermine, the Chain Link Mentorship System, democratic goal-setting via Adaption Reports + voting, and biological self-healing mechanisms.
- The Fitness Function (Fᵢ) is the primary bridge between economic performance and influence within the mentorship and governance layers.

## 2. Value Creation & Measurement

### 2.1 Primary Role Value
- Every Primary Role has defined **Contribution Metrics** (e.g., resources produced, infrastructure maintained, defense readiness provided, education outcomes delivered).
- Metrics are tracked through the existing Chain Link Progress Reports and aggregated upward.
- Value is assessed on **net contribution** after accounting for resources consumed.

### 2.2 Secondary Role (Passion Project) Value
- Passion projects generate **Retirement Points** when they produce measurable benefit to the colony (e.g., new tools, improved processes, cultural/artistic value, scientific insights, efficiency gains).
- A standardized but flexible **Value Assessment Framework** determines how many retirement points a project earns.
- High-value passion projects can temporarily increase a Quad’s or individual’s influence in relevant Adaption Reports and voting.

### 2.3 Aggregate Value at Higher Scales
- Academies and Networks report aggregated value creation upward through the Chain Link system.
- Colony-level value includes cross-Network trade efficiency, innovation propagation, and crisis resilience contributions.

---

## 3. Time Allocation System

### 3.1 Standard Split
- **70% Primary Role** / **30% Secondary (Passion)**
- This split is the default for all working-age citizens (age 18 to 59½).

### 3.2 Adjusted Splits
- **Parent Split (First 5 years of child’s life)**: 21% Primary / 9% Secondary / 70% Child-rearing (both parents).
- **Crisis / High-Need Adjustments**: Temporary reallocation of time toward critical Primary Roles during Tier 2+ resilience events (automatic via trigger system).

### 3.3 Passion Time as Earned Freedom
- High-performing Quads and individuals can earn **additional passion time** (up to 40–50%) as a direct reward for sustained high contribution.
- This is the primary non-material incentive in the system.

---

## 4. Internal Resource Distribution

### 4.1 Baseline Allocation (Collective)
- Every Quad receives a standard allocation of housing, food, clothing, tools, and healthcare that improves collectively as Academy productivity rises.
- Allocation is **need-based** with priority during crises (mycelial mother-tree principle).

### 4.2 Performance-Based Enhancements
- High-fitness Quads and Academies receive **priority access** to upgraded tools, better facilities for passion projects, and additional discretionary resources.
- These enhancements are temporary and must be maintained through continued performance (reinforcement logic).

### 4.3 Crisis Prioritization
- During Tier 2+ resilience events, resources are automatically redirected toward high-fitness surviving nodes (Crisis Survival Prioritization mechanism from Section 13).

---

## 5. Retirement Liquidity Pool Mechanics

### 5.1 Funding
- A fixed percentage (proposed starting point: 8–12%) of total Academy/Network production value is automatically diverted into the Retirement Liquidity Pool.
- This percentage can be adjusted through Colony-level democratic processes (Adaption Report + weighted voting).

### 5.2 Draw Rights
- Retired citizens (age 59½ or earlier via earned retirement points) can draw from the pool for **luxuries and quality-of-life upgrades** only.
- Draw amount and priority are calculated using a combination of:
  - Total retirement points earned over lifetime
  - Length and quality of service (Primary Role performance)
  - Demonstrated contribution through passion projects
  - Current Network/Colony prosperity level

### 5.3 Sustainability Rules
- Pool draws are capped as a percentage of current pool size to prevent depletion.
- High-performing Networks contribute more to the pool and therefore have higher average draw rights for their retirees.
- Automatic review triggers if pool draw rate exceeds sustainable thresholds for more than 3 cycles.

## 6. Incentive & Feedback Systems

### 6.1 Positive Reinforcement
- Successful Primary Role performance + high-value passion projects → stronger Development Links → higher influence in Adaption Reports and voting → priority resource access → faster retirement timeline.
- This creates a virtuous cycle aligned with the Fitness Function.

### 6.2 Negative Feedback (Pruning)
- Sustained low contribution or extractive behavior → weakened Development Links → reduced influence and resource priority → automatic shift/pruning of links if performance does not recover.
- This prevents free-riding without creating permanent punishment classes.

### 6.3 Alignment with Biological Principles
- The economic layer must amplify the existing reinforcement + pruning, rhythmic information flow, and need-based support mechanisms rather than override them with purely financial incentives.

---

## 7. Internal Trade & Exchange

### 7.1 Quad-to-Quad and Academy-to-Academy Trade
- Trade is voluntary and recorded through the Chain Link system.
- Most trade occurs in the form of **labor exchange, knowledge transfer, or resource sharing** rather than a universal internal currency.
- High-value or large-scale trades can be recorded on the lightweight ledger for transparency and dispute resolution.

### 7.2 Knowledge & Innovation Markets
- Successful adaptations and innovations from temporary splits or passion projects can be voluntarily shared across Quads and Academies.
- Originators receive retirement point bonuses and strengthened Development Links when their innovations are adopted widely.

### 7.3 No Debt or Interest
- The system does not use debt or interest as economic mechanisms. All resource flows are either collective allocations, performance-based enhancements, or voluntary exchange.

---

## 8. Cross-Academy / Cross-Network Economics

### 8.1 Inter-Academy Trade
- Academies can engage in voluntary trade of goods, services, knowledge, and personnel through their Development Links.
- Trade strengthens the relevant Development Links (positive feedback).

### 8.2 Inter-Network and Inter-Colony Trade
- Larger-scale trade follows the same principles but requires higher-level quorum approval for major resource movements.
- Strategic resource sharing during crises is governed by the resilience trigger system (Section 13).

### 8.3 Migration Economics
- Individuals or Quads moving between Academies carry their accumulated retirement points and performance history.
- Receiving Academies gain the benefit of incoming human capital but also inherit responsibility for integration through the Chain Link Mentorship system.

---

## 9. Economic Governance & Adaptation

### 9.1 Economic Adaption Reports
- Periodic Adaption Reports at each scale include economic health indicators (productivity trends, pool sustainability, incentive alignment effectiveness, inequality measures if any emerge).
- Major economic rule changes require democratic input via the existing Adaption Report + voting process.

### 9.2 Automatic Stabilizers
- The system includes built-in stabilizers (e.g., automatic increase in passion time rewards during high-productivity periods, temporary tightening of retirement draw rules during stress events).

### 9.3 Evolutionary Improvement
- Economic rules themselves are subject to the same reinforcement + pruning logic: successful policies strengthen and spread; ineffective ones are naturally deprioritized or formally pruned through democratic processes.

---

## 10. Anti-Gaming, Sustainability & Resilience Integration

### 10.1 Anti-Gaming Measures
- All value metrics and retirement point calculations are designed to be resistant to gaming through multi-factor assessment and cross-verification via the Chain Link Mentorship system.
- Sustained gaming behavior triggers automatic link weakening and investigation through the justice layer.

### 10.2 Integration with Resilience Triggers (Section 13)
- Economic mechanics automatically adjust during Tier 2+ events (e.g., increased focus on critical Primary Roles, temporary suspension of non-essential passion time, prioritized resource flow to high-fitness nodes).
- The Retirement Liquidity Pool has emergency draw restrictions during existential crises to preserve long-term sustainability.

### 10.3 Long-Term Sustainability
- Economic rules are evaluated not only on short-term productivity but also on their effect on long-term resilience, skill development, and civilizational adaptability.

## Summary of Key Design Goals

| Goal | How Economic Mechanics Support It |
|------|-----------------------------------|
| Maintain ant-level resilience | Automatic economic reallocation during crises + integration with Section 13 trigger system |
| Reinforce Chain Link Mentorship | Performance directly affects link strength and influence |
| Make "Time as Currency" real | Passion time is the primary earned reward; baseline needs are collective |
| Prevent free-riding without hierarchy | Automatic pruning of weak economic links + need-based support |
| Enable fractal scaling | Same logic works from Quad to Colony level with appropriate aggregation |
| Align incentives with biological principles | Reinforcement + pruning, rhythmic feedback, emergent hubs, and need-based flow are first-class economic mechanisms |
