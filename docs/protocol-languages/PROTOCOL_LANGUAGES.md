**Context**: Mapping from ASI/intent-engineering challenges (foreign intent injection, attention competition, Lyapunov stability) onto the Kiyoshi Protective Membrane (AS-0002 v0.3) and Anise cybernetic cell.

## Two Languages Required

1. **Internal Authority Language (IAL)**
   - Used only after Protective Membrane verification of the graded Available Authority Vector \( A_s(t) = (O, R, T, D, I, P, V) \).
   - Carries intent claims, Evidence Bus records, and verified state.
   - Must be contracting: acceptance cannot introduce positive Lyapunov exponents.

2. **Zero-Trust Transfer Language (ZTL)**
   - Used for all peer-to-peer and external messages before verification.
   - Claims are always treatable as Unknown; Membrane may reject, transform, contain, or isolate.
   - Never silently escalates to Safe.

Each language requires its own cryptographic suite for authenticity, integrity, and non-repudiation enforced by mathematics.
